Who We Are

Building Self-Reliant & Inclusive Communities

Rural Development Organization (RDO) is a non-profit NGO working to uplift underprivileged communities in rural areas. We bridge the gap by providing access to basic human needs, emergency relief during natural disasters, and sustainable livelihood tools.

  • Community-led development approach
  • Focus on women, children, and vulnerable groups
  • Commitment to accountability and transparency

WASH & Water Projects

Our Core Focus Areas

Addressing critical needs to foster long-term community growth.

Quality Education

Establishing community schools, offering scholarships, and raising awareness for child education.

WASH & Clean Water

Installing water pumps, deep wells, and educating remote communities on health & hygiene.

Basic Healthcare

Organizing free medical relief camps and maternal health awareness programs in rural areas.

Livelihood & Agriculture

Empowering smallholder farmers, providing skill development, and training rural artisans.

Disaster Relief

Emergency food ration distribution, shelter assistance, and flood recovery support.

Women Empowerment

Vocational training and financial independence initiatives for marginalized rural women.
