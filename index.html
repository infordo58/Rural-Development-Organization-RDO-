Strategic Horizon

Our Vision

"A prosperous society where people have the right to live with dignity, and all resources are equitably managed and sustained."

Operational Mandate

Our Mission

"To facilitate a Non-Political Movement for positive social change and community empowerment through Mass Literacy, Primary Healthcare, and Grassroots Economic Development in rural and urban areas of Pakistan."

Executive Directorate

Leadership Messages

Founding Leadership

Mr. Yar Muhammad Jamali

President & Executive Director

"At RDO, we believe sustainable development is only possible when communities actively participate in their own growth. Our commitment is rooted in integrity, mass literacy, and grassroots empowerment to ensure every individual lives with equity and dignity."

Under his visionary guidance, RDO bridges community needs with institutional governance, regulatory authorities, and international development frameworks.

Operations & Strategy

Mr. Anas Memon

Chief Operating Officer

"Our operational strength relies on rigorous policy standards—from PSEA and anti-fraud to transparent procurement and accounting. We ensure that every resource entrusted to RDO directly creates measurable impact on the ground."

Overseeing daily administration, project milestones, and logistics, the COO coordinates field interventions aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals.
