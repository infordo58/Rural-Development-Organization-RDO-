About RDO

Official Registration: Registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (Registration No. 0058) on December 31, 2024[cite: 1]. Fully compliant and registered with the Sindh Charity Commission[cite: 1].

Vision

A prosperous society where people have the right to live with dignity, and all resources are equitably managed and sustained[cite: 1].

Mission

To facilitate a Non-Political Movement for positive social change and community empowerment through Mass Literacy, Primary Healthcare, and Grassroots Economic Development in rural and urban areas of Pakistan[cite: 1].

Leadership Messages

Mr. Yar Muhammad Jamali

President & Executive Director

"At RDO, we believe sustainable development is only possible when communities actively participate in their own growth. Our commitment is rooted in integrity, mass literacy, and grassroots empowerment to ensure every individual lives with equity and dignity."

Under his vision, RDO operates as a non-political platform bridging local community needs with institutional governance, local authorities, and national regulatory compliance[cite: 1].

Mr. Anas Memon

COO

"Our operational strength relies on rigorous policy standards—from PSEA and anti-fraud to transparent procurement and accounting. We ensure that every resource entrusted to RDO directly creates measurable impact on the ground."

Overseeing daily management, administration, and program implementation, the COO ensures all grassroots initiatives align with our core governance manuals and UN Sustainable Development Goals[cite: 1].
